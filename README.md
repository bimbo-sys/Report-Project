# Sales Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Background
This project analyses retail sales transactions to understand business performance, identify profitability challenges and provide data-driven recommendations for improving sales and operational efficiency.

## 2. Project Objectives
- Analyse sales revenue, profit and profit margins.
- Identify high-performing and loss-making products.
- Compare branch and sales-channel performance.
- Examine customer purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Identify data-quality issues and recommend improvements.

## 3. Dataset Description
**Dataset:** `cleaned_data.csv`

- **Source:** Provided capstone sales dataset.
- **Period:** January 2024 – December 2025.
- **Records:** 11,297 rows.
- **Columns:** 22.
- **Key fields:** Order ID, Order Date, Customer ID, Product ID, Branch ID, Channel, Quantity, Discount, Sales Amount, Cost Amount and Profit.

## 4. Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning and preliminary analysis.
- **MySQL:** Data querying, aggregation and validation.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory analysis and visualisation.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and business reporting.

## 5. Methodology
1. Imported and inspected the sales dataset.
2. Identified missing values, duplicates and inconsistent records.
3. Cleaned and validated the data.
4. Modelled the data using fact and dimension tables.
5. Performed SQL queries and Python exploratory data analysis.
6. Compared key findings across analytical tools.
7. Developed Power BI dashboards to communicate insights.

## 6. Key Findings
- Total recorded revenue was approximately 771.90 million.
- Total recorded profit was approximately 104.17 million.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 13.50%.
- Revenue increased in 2025 compared with 2024, while profit margin declined.
- Some products generated negative profit.
- Sales channels and branches contributed differently to revenue and profitability.
- High discount levels were associated with profitability concerns.
- Data-quality issues affected some customer, branch and shipment records.



## 7. Recommendations
- Review pricing and costs of loss-making products.
- Strengthen discount approval and monitoring.
- Improve branch and sales-channel performance.
- Develop customer retention strategies.
- Improve data validation and reporting accuracy.
- Monitor revenue, profit margins and returns through Power BI dashboards.
